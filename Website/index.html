Core Concept

One game. Real rewards.
Zero friction.

Orbi is a mobile-first game built for iOS. Players pay a one-time 1 SOL entry fee to receive their Moon character — non-transferable, ensuring organic progression. From there, they play minigames, manage a farm, open chests, and trade in the marketplace. Every action flows through the native $STR token.

  • 🎮
    Daily Minigames — Moonbound and MoonCrush with more coming. Tiered leaderboard payouts in $STR every 24 hours.
  • 🌾
    On-chain Farming — Grow wheat → feed cows → produce milk → make cheese. Cheese replenishes the hunger bar to keep playing.
  • 📦
    Chest System — Common to Cosmic rarity. Proceeds fund the staking treasury, creating a self-sustaining economy.
  • 💱
    Marketplace — Trade accessories, cows & items peer-to-peer in SOL. Developers earn 1% on every transaction.
  • 🔒
    Staking — Lock $STR for 10, 20, or 30 days to earn 20%, 30%, or 50% of the treasury respectively — funded by chest revenue, not token inflation.

Sustainable Tokenomics

$STR launches via Raydium CLMM with LP tokens burned at launch. Controlled emission through deflationary burning, staking sinks, and chest-funded treasury rewards — not uncapped minting. Supply is fixed from day one.

Deflationary Staking Sink LP Burned
🛡️

Bot Resistance

Energy and hunger bars cap daily playable time, making botting economically unattractive. The non-transferable Moon character prevents Sybil attacks and multiple-account farming.

📈

Early Adopter Flywheel

The 1 SOL entry fee and XP-gated chest system rewards early players with better item access, creating genuine first-mover advantage and community FOMO.

🔄

Semi-Circular Economy

Chest purchases → staking treasury → rewards to stakers. The Marketplace (SOL) generates dev revenue. Two distinct revenue loops reinforce each other rather than competing.

Tokenomics

Why $STR, not SOL?

Using a native token creates a closed loop — SOL enters the ecosystem once, STR circulates indefinitely.

🔁

Circular Economy

Players earn $STR through minigames and spend it on chests, marketplace fees, and crafting. Every dollar stays inside the ecosystem rather than flowing out to SOL holders.

🔥

Deflationary by Design

A portion of $STR spent on chests and marketplace transactions is permanently burned, reducing supply over time and creating natural price support as the player base grows.

🚀

Raydium CLMM Launch

$STR launches on Raydium's concentrated liquidity market maker. LP tokens are burned at launch, ensuring permanent liquidity and eliminating rug-pull risk for early holders.

Why Orbi

The gap nobody else is filling

The mobile gaming world and the Web3 world don't yet talk to each other. Traditional mobile games are accessible and fun — but players spend real money on items they'll never own. The moment the game shuts down, everything disappears.

Web3 games promise real ownership — but overwhelm casual players with complexity, high entry costs, and gameplay loops that feel like work. The result: they appeal only to people already deep in crypto.

Orbi sits in that gap. An actually playable mobile game built on Solana, where every item, every trade, and every achievement is real on-chain ownership. With Solana's speed and near-zero transaction fees, the friction that held Web3 gaming back is gone. Orbi is built for the moment that's now arrived.
