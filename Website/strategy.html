Long-Term Strategy

Monetisation, Revenue
& Governance

How Orbi generates sustainable revenue, manages its token economy, and scales toward community governance.

Mission

Make crypto gaming accessible to everyone — not just degens.

Orbi exists to lower the barrier to on-chain gaming without sacrificing the genuine ownership and financial upside that makes crypto games compelling. One Solana. One Moon character. Infinite possibility.

Vision

Become the #1 casual play-to-earn game on Solana within 18 months.

Expand from iOS to Android. Grow the minigame library to 10+ titles. Establish a DAO governance structure where $STR holders vote on game updates, treasury allocation, and tokenomics parameters.

Monetisation

Four revenue streams

Orbi generates revenue from multiple complementary sources — none of which require token inflation.

🎟️
1 SOL

Player Registration

Every new player pays a one-time 1 SOL entry fee to receive their Moon character NFT. This is Orbi's first and most scalable revenue stream — purely driven by user growth. At 1,000 players: ~$150k (at $150/SOL). Non-dilutive, non-recurring.

One-time Scales with users Non-inflationary
💱
1%

Marketplace Fee

Every peer-to-peer trade in the Orbi Marketplace generates a 1% fee paid to the developer wallet. As item values appreciate and trade volume grows, this becomes a compounding passive income stream entirely in SOL. Enforced on-chain — uncircumventable.

Ongoing In SOL On-chain enforced
📦
Chest Sales

Treasury Funding via STR

Chest purchases in $STR flow directly into the staking treasury, funding yield for stakers. This creates a demand loop: more players → more chests → better staking yields → more players holding $STR → higher $STR price → more players attracted.

Flywheel STR Sink Funds staking
🌊
DLMM Fees

Liquidity Pool Earnings

Post-launch, a Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker (DLMM) pool on Raydium allows third parties to provide STR liquidity and earn trading fees. This deepens the order book, tightens spreads, and makes $STR more attractive to hold — benefiting all stakeholders.

Raydium DLMM Deep liquidity

Token Flow & Sinks

▸
Chest purchases — primary STR sink. Burns a portion, routes rest to staking treasury.
▸
Staking locks — tokens removed from circulation for fixed periods (30/90/180 days), reducing sell pressure.
▸
Leaderboard rewards — distributed daily from a fixed per-day STR budget, not minted fresh.
▸
Dev supply control — team can adjust mini-game rewards per epoch to stabilise STR price without touching supply.
Token Launch

Raydium CLMM → DLMM

The initial token release uses a Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker (CLMM) pool on Raydium. This lets the team set the initial market cap and price range without deploying large capital.

LP tokens are burned immediately — provably locked liquidity. 1–3 days post-launch, a DLMM pool opens so third parties can provide liquidity and earn fees, creating a decentralised, self-sustaining market.

Governance

Progressively decentralising control

We launch with a centralised team structure for speed and ship quality, then hand control to $STR holders as the ecosystem matures.

🏛️

Phase 1–3: Team-Led

Development, tokenomics parameters, and treasury allocation are controlled by the founding team. This allows fast iteration, security response, and coherent product vision during the critical early phase. All decisions are announced publicly on X and Discord before execution.

🗳️

Phase 4: $STR DAO

Once the ecosystem is stable (5,000+ active players), governance transitions to a token-weighted DAO. $STR holders vote on: new minigame additions, staking rate adjustments, treasury spend proposals, and protocol upgrades. Voting power scales with staking duration — long-term holders get stronger votes.

🔐

Multisig Treasury

From day one, the development treasury is controlled by a 3-of-5 Squads multisig. No single team member can move funds unilaterally. Addresses are published publicly so the community can monitor all treasury activity on-chain.

⚡

Program Upgrade Authority

The Solana program's upgrade authority will be transferred from the team wallet to the multisig after the mainnet launch, and eventually to a DAO-controlled PDA. This timeline is committed publicly and enforced by the community.

Risk Management

We know the risks. Here's how we mitigate them.

Risk Level Mitigation
Token price collapse (Axie Infinity scenario) High Fixed supply, staking sinks, no uncapped minting, chest-funded rewards
Bot farm exploitation Medium Energy/hunger caps enforced on-chain + API; non-transferable Moon NFT prevents multi-accounting
Smart contract exploit High Third-party Anchor audit before mainnet; devnet testing period; bug bounty programme
Regulatory uncertainty (crypto games) Medium $STR is a utility token for gameplay, not marketed as investment. Legal counsel engaged pre-launch.
Low player retention Low Proven casual game loop (farming, minigames, progression) with financial upside as added incentive
Solana network outages Low Game continues in read-only mode during outages; farm timers pause gracefully; no funds at risk
