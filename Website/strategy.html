Token Creation

How $STR is minted

Transparent, fixed, and verifiable on-chain from day one.

The minting process

1
SPL Token created on Solana
$STR is a standard Solana Program Library (SPL) token. The mint authority is set at deployment with a fixed total supply — no additional tokens can ever be created.
2
Mint authority revoked
Immediately after the full supply is minted, the mint authority is permanently revoked on-chain. No wallet — including the developer wallet — can ever mint more $STR.
3
Supply distributed per allocation
Tokens are distributed to wallets matching the allocation table: 40% to the staking treasury smart contract, 30% to the Raydium CLMM pool, 15% to the team vesting contract, 10% to the airdrop/marketing wallet, 5% to reserve.

Supply policy

Total Supply Fixed at mint

Deflationary mechanism ~1% burned per chest purchase

Trust & Transparency

We won't rug pull.
Here's the proof.

The cypherpunk ethos is simple: don't trust, verify. Every commitment we make is enforceable on-chain — not just a promise.

🔥

LP Tokens Burned

At launch, the $STR/SOL liquidity pool LP tokens are burned on-chain. This permanently removes the ability for anyone — including us — to withdraw the initial liquidity. The pool is locked forever. Any wallet can verify this on Solscan the moment it happens.

🔐

3-Signature Multisig Treasury

The development treasury is controlled by a 3-of-3 Squads multisig from day one. No single developer can move funds unilaterally. All three wallet addresses are published publicly — the community can monitor every treasury transaction on-chain in real time.

🧾

Smart Contract Audit

All Anchor smart contracts are audited by a third-party security firm (targeting CertiK or equivalent) before mainnet launch. The full audit report is published publicly. No mainnet launch until the audit passes — this is non-negotiable.

📊

Independent Economic Audit

Prior to mainnet, we commission an independent economic audit of the $STR treasury model — similar to the approach taken by Gauntlet for DeFi protocols. This verifies that staking reward rates, chest burn mechanics, and treasury projections are sustainable at scale. Results published openly.

🎲

On-Chain Rarity Rolls

Item rarity from chest openings is determined on-chain — not by a server we control. Players can verify every drop is fair. We cannot manipulate drop rates after deployment without a published contract upgrade, which requires multisig approval.

🌙

Non-Transferable Moon Character

The Moon character NFT is soul-bound to the player's wallet. The developer team cannot mint additional characters to their own wallets to farm rewards. One wallet, one Moon, organic progression only — enforced at the contract level.

Token Flow & Sinks

▸
Chest purchases — primary STR sink. Burns a portion, routes rest to staking treasury.
▸
Staking locks — tokens removed from circulation for fixed periods (30/90/180 days), reducing sell pressure.
▸
Leaderboard rewards — distributed daily from a fixed per-day STR budget, not minted fresh.
▸
Dev supply control — team can adjust mini-game rewards per epoch to stabilise STR price without touching supply.
Token Launch

Raydium CLMM → DLMM

The initial token release uses a Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker (CLMM) pool on Raydium. This lets the team set the initial market cap and price range without deploying large capital.

LP tokens are burned immediately — provably locked liquidity. 1–3 days post-launch, a DLMM pool opens so third parties can provide liquidity and earn fees, creating a decentralised, self-sustaining market.

Governance

Progressively Decentralised Control

We launch with a developer-led structure for speed and quality, then give $STR holders a formal voice as the ecosystem matures — without surrendering product control.

🏛️

Phase 1–3: Developer-Led

Development, tokenomics parameters, and treasury allocation are controlled by the developers. This allows fast iteration, security response, and coherent product vision during the critical early phase. All decisions are announced publicly on X and Discord before execution.

🗳️

Phase 4: Token Holder Governance

Once the ecosystem is stable, $STR holders gain the ability to formally oppose proposed changes to game structure, staking rates, treasury allocation, and tokenomics parameters. Developers retain final authority through weighted voting rights — ensuring the community has a real voice without the product losing direction. Long-term stakers receive stronger opposition weight than short-term holders.

🔐

3-Signature Multisig Treasury

From day one, the development treasury is controlled by a 3-signature Squads multisig — all three signatures are required to move any funds. No single developer can act unilaterally. Wallet addresses are published publicly so the community can monitor all treasury activity on-chain.
