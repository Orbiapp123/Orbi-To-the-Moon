Monetisation

Three revenue streams

Orbi generates revenue from multiple complementary sources — none of which require token inflation.

🎟️
1 SOL

Player Registration

Every new player pays a one-time entry fee to receive their Moon character NFT. This is Orbi's first and most scalable revenue stream — purely driven by user growth. At 1,000 players: ~$150k (at $150/SOL). Non-dilutive, non-recurring.

📌 Launch note: The entry fee will be set below 1 SOL initially to encourage early adoption and build a player base quickly. Once 200 players have registered, the fee increases to the full 1 SOL — rewarding early adopters and creating a natural first-mover incentive.

Marketplace Fee

Every peer-to-peer trade in the Orbi Marketplace generates a 1% fee paid to the developer wallet. As item values appreciate and trade volume grows, this becomes a compounding revenue stream entirely in SOL. Enforced on-chain — uncircumventable.

Token Flow & Sinks

▸
Chest purchases — primary STR sink. Burns a portion, routes rest to staking treasury.
▸
Staking locks — tokens removed from circulation for fixed periods (30/90/180 days), reducing sell pressure.
▸
Leaderboard rewards — distributed daily from a fixed per-day STR budget, not minted fresh.
▸
Dev supply control — team can adjust mini-game rewards per epoch to stabilise STR price without touching supply.
Token Launch

Raydium CLMM → DLMM

The initial token release uses a Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker (CLMM) pool on Raydium. This lets the team set the initial market cap and price range without deploying large capital.

LP tokens are burned immediately — provably locked liquidity. 1–3 days post-launch, a DLMM pool opens so third parties can provide liquidity and earn fees, creating a decentralised, self-sustaining market.

Governance

Progressively Decentralised Control

We launch with a developer-led structure for speed and quality, then give $STR holders a formal voice as the ecosystem matures — without surrendering product control.

🏛️

Phase 1–3: Developer-Led

Development, tokenomics parameters, and treasury allocation are controlled by the developers. This allows fast iteration, security response, and coherent product vision during the critical early phase. All decisions are announced publicly on X and Discord before execution.

🗳️

Phase 4: Token Holder Governance

Once the ecosystem is stable, $STR holders gain the ability to formally oppose proposed changes to game structure, staking rates, treasury allocation, and tokenomics parameters. Developers retain final authority through weighted voting rights — ensuring the community has a real voice without the product losing direction. Long-term stakers receive stronger opposition weight than short-term holders.

🔐

3-Signature Multisig Treasury

From day one, the development treasury is controlled by a 3-signature Squads multisig — all three signatures are required to move any funds. No single developer can act unilaterally. Wallet addresses are published publicly so the community can monitor all treasury activity on-chain.
