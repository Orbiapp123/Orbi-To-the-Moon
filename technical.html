Game Mechanics

How the game actually works

Six interlocking systems that create a self-sustaining play loop — every mechanic feeds the next, and every action has an on-chain consequence.

🌙

The Moon Character

Every player owns exactly one Moon character NFT — soul-bound to their wallet. It cannot be transferred, bought, or duplicated. The Moon levels up as you play, unlocking new farming plots, higher leaderboard tiers, and exclusive chest access. Accessories collected from chests or the marketplace are equipped to your Moon, giving it a look that reflects your rarity. One wallet. One Moon. Organic progression only.

Soul-bound NFT XP system Cosmetic accessories
🌾

Farming

The farming loop is: plant seeds → grow wheat → feed cows → collect milk → make cheese. Each step is a timed action validated by the backend — you can't harvest before the timer expires, and you can't skip steps. Cows have rarity tiers that affect milk yield. Farm produce can be sold peer-to-peer on the marketplace for SOL, creating a genuine off-chain value loop. Seed Chests unlock additional farm plots, giving long-term players a compounding farming advantage.

Timed actions Sell for SOL Cow rarity tiers
🎮

Minigames

Minigames are skill-based arcade games embedded in the app. Each play costs energy and scores points on the daily leaderboard. The leaderboard resets at midnight UTC — every day is a fresh race. Top-ranked players share a fixed daily $STR reward pool, paid from the treasury. This pool is not minted fresh — it is drawn from the 40% staking treasury allocation, meaning every reward is backed by real supply. More minigames roll out via community vote in Phase 4.

Daily leaderboard Fixed STR budget Skill-based
⚡

Energy & Hunger System

Every game action — farming a plot, playing a minigame — costs energy. Energy restores passively over real time. Your Moon also has a hunger bar that depletes slowly; a hungry Moon earns less STR per action. Both bars are hard-capped and enforced at the API level and on-chain via timestamp checks. This is the core anti-bot mechanism: no wallet can drain the treasury through automation. A bot plays at the same rate as a human — the cap removes the advantage entirely.

Bot-resistant On-chain enforced Passive restore
🏆

Leaderboard & $STR Rewards

The daily leaderboard ranks players by their combined farming output and minigame score. Top players at midnight UTC receive $STR rewards — split proportionally across the top tier. To prevent exploitation, referred players must reach a minimum level before their referrer earns a bonus, eliminating fake-account farming. Leaderboard rewards are intentionally modest at launch — enough to be compelling, not enough to inflate the supply dangerously. Developer-adjustable per epoch to stabilise price.

Daily reset Anti-exploit gating Epoch-adjustable
🛒

Marketplace

Players list items — accessories, cows, seeds — for peer-to-peer sale in SOL. When a sale completes, 99% goes to the seller and 1% to the developer wallet. This is enforced by the smart contract: the item is held in a PDA escrow and only released when payment clears. Rarity drives price discovery naturally — Cosmic accessories command multiples of Common ones. The marketplace creates a genuine economy inside the game: items have real SOL value, and players with rare chests have a real financial edge.

PDA escrow SOL-denominated 1% dev fee

Layer 1

Frontend

A lightweight, no-framework stack keeps the game fast, auditable, and easy to embed in an iOS WebView wrapper. That's what we need from a professional developer — take this prototype and wrap it in a native iOS shell.

Wallet Integration

The Phantom Wallet React Native SDK provides a window.solana object. Users connect once and sign transactions without leaving the app. All key management stays in Phantom — Orbi never touches private keys.

When a user connects, the SDK returns their public key — a unique wallet address that becomes their player ID throughout the entire game. The private key never leaves their device. Because the app is built in JavaScript, the same codebase runs on both iOS and Android — no rewrite needed when we expand.

Every action that touches real value — buying a chest, listing a marketplace item, staking STR — triggers a Phantom wallet signature prompt. The user approves it in Phantom, the signed transaction is broadcast to Solana, and the result is confirmed on-chain before the game UI updates. The game never assumes a transaction succeeded — it waits for on-chain confirmation.

What is an iOS WebView Wrapper?

An iOS WebView wrapper is a native iPhone app shell that loads a web app inside it. Instead of rebuilding the game in Swift, you create an iOS container with a WebView component, and that WebView loads Orbi. The user interacts with a normal-feeling iPhone app — they'd never know it's a website inside. This is the standard approach for shipping a web prototype on the App Store.

Layer 2

Python Backend

The Python backend responds quickly to app requests, while another background process watches the blockchain and updates the app's stored data. FastAPI serves low-latency REST endpoints; a separate background indexer listens to Solana events and keeps the database in sync.

What is REST?

REST is a standard way of designing an API so the frontend can talk to the backend through clear URL-based endpoints. Each endpoint does a specific job, and standard HTTP methods are used to show the type of action: for example, GET fetches data and POST sends or triggers an action. In Orbi, routes like /player/{wallet} or /leaderboard are REST API endpoints, and the backend returns the requested information, usually in JSON format.

Responsibilities

  • ▸ Index on-chain events (chest purchases, marketplace trades, staking deposits)
  • ▸ Store item metadata: rarity, accessories, chest type, owner history
  • ▸ Compute and cache leaderboard rankings — updated every 6 hours
  • ▸ Serve player profiles, inventory, energy/hunger bar state
  • ▸ Validate farm state transitions (wheat → milk → cheese logic)

Layer 3

Solana Smart Contract (Rust + Anchor)

All game logic that involves real value — registration, chest purchase, marketplace trades, staking — lives on-chain. It's important to understand the distinction between the two technologies used here:

What is Rust?

Rust is the programming language the smart contract is written in. It's chosen because it compiles to extremely efficient bytecode that runs on Solana, and its type system prevents entire categories of bugs — like memory corruption — at compile time. Rust is what guarantees the contract can't be tampered with or exploited at the language level.

What is Anchor?

Anchor is a framework built on top of Rust that makes writing Solana programs much faster and safer. It handles the boilerplate of account validation, serialisation, and error handling through simple macros like #[account] and #[program]. Think of Rust as the engine and Anchor as the framework that gives it structure — the way React is built on JavaScript.

On-chain Instructions

register_player
Collects the 1 SOL entry fee from the player's wallet and transfers it to the dev treasury. Mints the Moon character as a soul-bound NFT to the player's wallet — one per wallet, enforced at the contract level. Initialises the player account on-chain with level 1, XP 0, full energy and hunger.
buy_chest
Burns the STR cost of the chest (~1% is permanently destroyed), routes the remainder to the staking treasury, and rolls item rarity on-chain using a verifiable random seed. The resulting item NFT is minted to the player's wallet. Rarity is immutable — it cannot be changed after minting.
list_item
Transfers the item NFT into a Program Derived Address (PDA) escrow vault owned exclusively by the smart contract. The player sets a SOL price. The item is held safely — the seller cannot withdraw it, and the buyer cannot take it without paying. Neither party can be rugged.
buy_item
Atomically: transfers 99% of the SOL price to the seller, 1% to the developer wallet, and releases the NFT from escrow to the buyer — all in a single transaction. If any step fails, the whole transaction reverts. There is no state in which payment is made but the item is not delivered.
stake_str
Locks the chosen STR amount in a PDA vault, records the unlock timestamp (current time + lock duration) and the applicable yield rate for the selected tier. The PDA has no private key — only the contract logic can release it. Even the developer cannot move staked tokens before unlock.
claim_rewards
Available only after the unlock timestamp is reached. Verifies the clock, calculates yield earned for the exact duration staked, transfers principal + yield from the staking treasury to the player's wallet, and closes the PDA — returning its rent lamports to the player.

Security

Security-first by design

Every architectural decision was made with the threat model in mind.

1

Non-custodial Wallets

Orbi never stores or requests private keys. All signing happens inside Phantom on the user's device. The backend only ever sees public keys and on-chain events.

2

PDA Escrow for Marketplace

Listed items are held in a Program Derived Address (PDA) — not the developer wallet. Smart contract logic enforces that only the buyer's payment releases the NFT. No rug possible.

3

Anchor Constraint Checks

Anchor's #[account(mut, has_one = owner)] constraints prevent any wallet from acting on another player's accounts. All authority checks are compile-time enforced.

4

Locked Liquidity — Raydium CLMM

$STR launches on Raydium's Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker (CLMM) — Solana's leading DEX. At launch, the initial SOL + $STR liquidity is deposited into the pool and the resulting LP (Liquidity Provider) tokens are permanently burned. Burning LP tokens means no one — including the developers — can ever withdraw the underlying liquidity. This is fully verifiable on-chain, eliminating rug-pull risk entirely. CLMM allows liquidity to be concentrated in specific price ranges, meaning tighter spreads and less slippage for traders compared to a standard AMM pool.

5

Bot Mitigation

Energy/hunger bar limits are enforced both in the backend (API rate limits) and on-chain (timestamp checks in smart contract state). Bots gain no economic advantage within the cap.

Token Launch

$STR Token Release, Liquidity & Supply

How $STR enters the market, how liquidity is structured, and how players are protected from day one.

🚀

Phase 1 — Initial Launch on Raydium CLMM

The initial release of $STR will be done on Raydium using a CLMM (Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker) pool. This allows us to set our initial market cap without having to put forward large amounts of capital — liquidity is deployed within a chosen price range rather than across the entire curve, making it far more capital-efficient.

At launch, LP (Liquidity Provider) tokens are burned. LP tokens represent ownership of the underlying pool assets. By burning them, no one — including the developers — can ever withdraw the initial liquidity. This is permanently verifiable on-chain.

This gives players a verifiable guarantee that Orbi cannot be rugged at the liquidity level. The pool stays open, the tokens stay tradeable, and the devs cannot pull the floor out.

Raydium CLMM LP Burned at Launch Capital-Efficient
💧

Phase 2 — DLMM & Community Liquidity (1–3 Days Post-Launch)

Shortly after launch, a DLMM (Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker) pool will be created. Unlike a standard CLMM, a DLMM adjusts its bin structure dynamically as the price moves, reducing impermanent loss for liquidity providers.

This pool is open to third parties and Orbi players — anyone can deposit their $STR into the pool and earn trading fees in return. This creates a powerful flywheel: players who hold $STR now have an additional reason to keep it rather than sell, since they can put it to work earning fees.

The more players participate, the deeper the liquidity, the tighter the spreads, and the harder it becomes for any single actor to move the price — further stabilising $STR for the entire ecosystem.

$STR Staking

Lock, Earn, and Hold

Staking is a core $STR sink. Players lock tokens into a Solana PDA for a fixed period and earn yield paid entirely from the staking treasury — no new minting, ever.

How Staking Works On-Chain

1
Player calls stake_str
The chosen amount of $STR is transferred into a Program Derived Address (PDA) owned exclusively by the smart contract. The unlock timestamp and applicable yield rate are written on-chain at this moment.
2
Tokens are locked
The PDA is a vault with no private key — only the smart contract logic can release it. Neither the player, nor the developer, can move the tokens before the unlock time. The contract enforces this absolutely.
3
Player calls claim_rewards
After the lock period expires, the player claims their original stake plus yield. The smart contract verifies the timestamp, transfers $STR from the staking treasury to the player, and closes the PDA.

Why staking is central to the token model

Staking is not an afterthought — it's the primary mechanism keeping $STR from hyperinflating. The design is deliberately borrowed from lessons learned from Axie Infinity's collapse.

📉

What Axie Infinity got wrong

Axie Infinity minted new SLP tokens to pay rewards with every game action. More players → more minting → more supply → token price collapses. There was no meaningful mechanism to remove tokens from circulation. Staking existed but paid rewards via fresh minting — the very thing that caused the death spiral. Within 18 months, SLP fell 99% from its peak.

📈

What Orbi does differently

$STR has a fixed supply — no minting after launch. Staking rewards come entirely from the 40% treasury allocation, not new tokens. Chest purchases burn ~1% of every transaction and route the remainder to the treasury. This means the game generates its own staking fuel through real player spend — not inflation. The treasury runs for years before depletion even at full player load.

Chest Purchases Refuel the Treasury

Every chest a player buys with $STR deposits the majority (post-burn) into the staking treasury. As the player base grows and chest demand rises, the treasury refills faster — staking yields become more sustainable, not less. Growth is self-reinforcing rather than inflationary.

🔒

Locks Contract Circulating Supply

Every staked token is out of circulation for the duration of the lock. A player staking 10,000 STR for 180 days removes that supply for 6 months. At scale — say 30% of all STR staked in long positions — the effective float contracts sharply, creating natural upward price pressure without any artificial mechanism.

⚖️

Governance Weight for Long Stakers

In Phase 4, staking tier determines how much weight your opposition vote carries. A 180-day staker has the most at stake in Orbi's long-term direction — so they get the most say. Short-term holders carry less weight. This aligns governance with the people who are most committed to the project's success.
